# Demo timed transcripts

The RealityOps V2 pump, living-room, and Emergency City V3/V4 masters are
silent-first: their burned-in story copy, proof chips, interaction cues, and
scene motion carry the complete story. Landscape and portrait editions use
separate tracks where their timelines differ. No timed-caption file is required
to understand any master.

The `.srt` files in this directory are optional translation/accessibility
transcripts. Do not burn a same-language transcript over the published master;
that would duplicate the existing visual copy and may cover real scene controls
in players that choose their own subtitle position. For a localized social
export, replace the burned-in copy in the corresponding Remotion composition
and use the SRT as its timing source.
